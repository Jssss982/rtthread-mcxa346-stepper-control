Integration changes to the upstream BSP:
  rtconfig.py: set GCC C startup to RT-Thread entry and clear BSS.
  board/linker_scripts/MCXA346_flash.ld: retain FSymTab, VSymTab and init tables.
  SConstruct: register the linker script dependency.
  applications/stepper: project application; packages/SConscript: package integration.
  tools/prepare-bsp.py reproduces the three GCC fixes.
